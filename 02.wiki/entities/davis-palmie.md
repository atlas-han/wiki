# Davis Palmie

[[factory-ai|Factory]]의 member of technical staff. [[tech-bridge-factory-bug-report-to-production]]에서 자기소개했다: *"I'm Davis Palmey, uh member of technical staff here at Factory. Uh before this, I co-founded Lumetric, which was an agent platform for investment teams that we took through Y Combinator and was acquired by Factory. And prior to that, I was a engineering lead for Slalom's Innovation Lab."*(00:00~00:18) **철자 Palmie는 설명란에만 있다**(en-orig *"Palmey"*). 이 위키 첫 등장이고, 이 위키의 두 번째 Factory 화자다(첫째는 [[tereza-tizkova|Tereza Tížková]]). 링크는 설명란에서 가져왔고 열어 보지 않았다.
