# Warp

**에이전트가 내장된 터미널에서 출발한 오픈소스 agentic development environment이자 그것을 만드는 회사.** 창업자 [[zach-lloyd|Zach Lloyd]]가 [[tech-bridge-warp-factory-engineering]]에서 소개했다. 본 위키 첫 등장.

> *"Warp, if you're not familiar, is a open source agentic development environment. You may know us as a terminal. That is how the company started. We're basically a terminal that has agents built in."* (00:36~00:51)
